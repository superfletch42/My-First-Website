The conservation of red pandas

What are the main threats to red pandas?

  • Habitat loss and Deforestation
  • Poaching and smuggling
  • Humans encroaching on their habitat

What is being done to stop it?

Red pandas have been recognised as an endangered species. It is outlawed to hunt red pandas as well as own them as pets. Most zoos nows house red pandas in an effort to raise their species numbers. There is an estimated 10,000 left in the wild.

Diet of the Red Panda

Table of their diet.
Food Type Description Notes
Bamboo Primary food source, consisting of bamboo leaves and shoots. Despite being a carnivore red pandas have a specialised bamboo diet.
Fruits, Berries and Nuts Red pandas have been seen eating Apples, berries and acorns. These sources are seasonal and are eaten when available.
Eggs Red pandas eat bird eggs when they come across them. Eggs provide additional protein and are often found in their diet during the breeding season.
Birds, Rodents and Lizards Occasionally, pregnant females have been seen hunting birds, rodents and lizards. This is not a major part of their diet but provides protein.
Insects Insects like grubs and beetles. Insects are a good source of protein, particularly during warmer months.
Grasses and flowers Red pandas sometimes eat grasses and flowers. These are supplementary foods when bamboo is scarce or not available.
