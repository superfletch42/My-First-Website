What Is a Red Panda?

Red Pandas are small mammals found in the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China. They have orange-brownish fur that acts as a camouflage, helping them blend in with the red moss and white lichen covered trees. The black fur on their stomach and legs makes it difficult for them to be spotted from below.

Taken by Alexander Van Steenberge from Unsplash

Fun Facts

  • Red pandas are the original Panda. They are called "firefox".
  • They primarily eat bamboo, even though they're classified as carnivores.
  • They use their bushy tails for balance and warmth.
  • They are excellent climbers and can descend trees head-first.
  • Despite wrong opinions, they are the cutest animal on Earth

Taken by Will Rust on Unsplash

Picture from Pexels

Why Are Red Pandas Endangered?

Habitat loss, poaching, and climate change have all contributed to declining red panda populations. Conservation efforts are ongoing, but they still remain a vulnerable species.
